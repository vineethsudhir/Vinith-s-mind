## Perks of being young.

I am a 23 year old at the moment, one of the perks of my current age is the freedom I have. I have the freedom to take impulsive decisions. I can do whatever I want to do right away. I do not know till when I will be able to enjoy this freedom. In my opinion, it is foolish to not make good use of this freedom.

I definately do not believe that we should start being reckless just because we are free. But we let the freedom create more opportunities for us.

In February of 2022, I felt like I was not making good use of my time. I am living so close to New York City. It is such a happening place. I was living is such a vibrant location yet lived a very boring monotonous life. I felt like I didn't experiment enough. I felt like I didn't get as much social interaction as I liked. 

I decided that It was time I took charge and live a fuller life. I was time I started doing things I enjoy. Most of the things my classmates in university considered fun were not something I particularly enjoyed. I didn't enjoy staying up late at night having debates over drinks. I didn't enjoy going for a movie every weekend. Though I enjoyed having meals with a large group, I generally didn't eat dinners (the only meal my classmates from university ate was a very late night dinner) as I liked waking up on a light stomach.

I enjoy exploring places and taking long walks. I enjoy going for long runs with big groups of people. I enjoy hanging out in pubs and meeting new people there. I felt like I should start doing things that I enjoy and use my freedom to do new fun things that makes me happy. I had to make decisions in the spur of the moment to have a great chance of doing things I find fun. 

Realizing the perks of the freedom that comes with my age allowed me to find new ways to do things I enjoy. I started being happier because I started having more fun in life when I started to participate in activities I saw on the internet and thought I would enjoy.
